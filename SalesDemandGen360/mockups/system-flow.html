01 — End to end

From an imported list to a sent message

Everything above the freeze line is ours and can be changed. Everything below it belongs to La Growth Machine and cannot. The line is not a policy — it is where content stops being editable, because LGM reads copy from the lead record rather than from us at send time.

02 — The interface

Where the two systems actually meet

One narrow join: we write numbered variables onto a lead, and LGM's steps read them. Everything about the division of labour follows from this being the only channel.

03 — Before and after

What a new signal can still change

The same event — a rep learns something material — has completely different reach depending on which side of enrolment the account sits.

Why this is not a limitation to design around. A cadence whose copy silently changes underneath an approved sequence would be worse. Freezing at enrolment means the words a rep approved are exactly the words that go out — which is the property the whole approval gate exists to guarantee.
