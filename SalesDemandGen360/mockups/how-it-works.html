Sales Demand Gen360 · Prospect Engine

How the whole thing works

Gen360 is marketing intelligence first: it discovers what is true about a company and the people in it, turns that into evidence-backed signals, and uses them to decide who to approach, why, and in what words. La Growth Machine does the sending. Eleven stages, end to end.
